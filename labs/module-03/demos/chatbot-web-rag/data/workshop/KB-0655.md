# Pairing tyre pressure sensors after a tyre change

## Symptom

After a tyre or wheel change, the tyre pressure monitoring system (TPMS) shows dashes for one
position, and fault code **FC-TPM-0120** (sensor not paired) is stored.

## Procedure

1. Check that the new sensor is the approved 433 MHz type and that its battery is not
   older than 6 years.
2. Select "TPMS > pair sensors" in DiagLink, then choose the axle and the wheel position.
3. Hold the TPMS activation tool against the tyre sidewall at the valve until the tool beeps.
4. Repeat for every changed position, then confirm the list in DiagLink.
5. Drive for at least 10 minutes above 25 km/h. The system then confirms the pairing, and the
   pressure values appear in the cluster.

## Notes

Twin tyres need the inner and outer positions paired separately. A sensor paired to the wrong
position shows pressures from the other wheel, so check the positions in DiagLink before the
vehicle leaves the workshop.
