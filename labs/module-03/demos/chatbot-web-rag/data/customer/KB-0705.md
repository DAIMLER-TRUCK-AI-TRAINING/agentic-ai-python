# The AdBlue warning lamp is on. What should the driver do?

AdBlue is the exhaust fluid that keeps your diesel truck's emissions within the legal limits.
The engine reduces its power when the system cannot work properly.

## Yellow lamp, steady

- **Tank nearly empty:** refill with AdBlue (ISO 22241) at the next opportunity.
- **Tank full:** the system has detected a fault. You can continue your trip, but book a
  workshop visit within the next few days.

## Yellow lamp, flashing

Engine power will be reduced soon. Drive to a safe place or to a workshop. If the fault is not
repaired, the truck limits its speed to 20 km/h after the next engine stop, so plan the stop
carefully.

## Never do this

- Never dilute AdBlue with water or fill it into the diesel tank.
- Never use AdBlue that has been stored in open containers.

## Need help on the road?

Call roadside assistance using the number on the windscreen sticker or in the Fleet Insight
app. Have the vehicle identification number (VIN) and the message shown in the cluster ready.
