# Roadside assistance: what to have ready when you call

Roadside assistance is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The number is on the
windscreen sticker and in the Fleet Insight app.

## Have this ready

1. **Vehicle identification number (VIN)**, from the vehicle documents or the Fleet Insight app.
2. **Exact location:** motorway, direction and the nearest junction or kilometre marker. The
   app can share the position for you.
3. **What the instrument cluster shows:** the warning lamp colour and the message text. If a
   fault code is displayed, read it out.
4. **Load and trailer:** whether the truck is loaded, and whether dangerous goods are on board.

## While you wait

Switch on the hazard lights, wear the high-visibility vest, and place the warning triangle at a
safe distance. On a motorway, wait behind the safety barrier.

## Costs

Roadside assistance is free during the warranty period and for fleets with a service contract.
Otherwise the attending workshop charges for the call-out and the repair.
