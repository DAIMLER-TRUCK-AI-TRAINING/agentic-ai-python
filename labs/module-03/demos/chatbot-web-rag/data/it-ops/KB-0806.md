# Warranty claim upload fails with "413 Payload Too Large"

## Symptom

Submitting a warranty claim in the Dealer Workshop Portal fails with the error
**413 Payload Too Large**, usually when photos of the damaged part are attached.

## Cause

One claim submission may carry at most **20 MB** of attachments. Modern phone photos are
4 to 8 MB each, so three or four photos already exceed the limit.

## Resolution

1. Resize photos to a maximum of **2,048 pixels** on the long side before attaching them. The
   portal's photo picker offers "Reduce size" for this.
2. Attach the DiagLink session report as PDF, not as a screenshot.
3. If the evidence still exceeds 20 MB, submit the claim with the essential photos and add the
   rest afterwards under "Claim > Add documents"; each addition has its own 20 MB limit.

Claims rejected for size are not saved as drafts, so copy long repair descriptions before you
submit.
