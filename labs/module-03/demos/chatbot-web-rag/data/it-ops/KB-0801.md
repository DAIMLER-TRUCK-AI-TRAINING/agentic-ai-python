# Dealer Workshop Portal sign-in fails after a password change

## Symptom

A dealer user changed their password and now cannot sign in to the Dealer Workshop Portal
(DWP). The sign-in page loops back to itself, or shows "Your session has expired" straight
after a successful password entry.

## Cause

The portal uses single sign-on. Browsers and the DiagLink desktop app keep a session token from
before the password change. The directory also needs up to 15 minutes to sync the new password
to the portal.

## Resolution

1. Wait **15 minutes** after the password change.
2. Sign out of every DWP session, including DiagLink ("Account > Sign out on this device").
3. Clear the browser cache and cookies for the portal, or use a private window.
4. Sign in again with the new password.

If the loop continues after these steps, the account may be locked after repeated attempts.
Raise an incident with the Service Desk under the category "Dealer applications" and include
the dealer number and the time of the last attempt. Never send the password itself.
