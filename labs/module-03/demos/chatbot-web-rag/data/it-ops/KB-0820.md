# Plant MES line terminal shows "order queue empty" after network maintenance

## Symptom

After planned switch maintenance in assembly hall 2 of plant EU-2, one or more MES line
terminals show "order queue empty", although the line has open production orders. Operators
cannot confirm assembly steps.

## Cause

The terminals keep their connection to the MES server open. When the access switches restart,
the MES client service does not reconnect by itself, and terminals that rejoin the wrong VLAN
cannot reach the server at all.

## Resolution

1. Check that the terminal has an address in the production VLAN **214**. If not, ask Plant
   network operations to correct the switch port.
2. Restart the "MES Client" service on the terminal from the plant support console. A restart
   takes about one minute; the line does not need to stop.
3. Confirm that the order queue is filled and that the operator can confirm a step.

If more than five terminals are affected, treat it as a P2 incident for the line and inform the
shift lead: confirmation gaps delay vehicle release at the end of the line.
