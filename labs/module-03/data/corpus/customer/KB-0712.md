# Understanding uptime alerts in the Fleet Insight portal

Fleet Insight watches the health of every connected truck in your fleet and sends an alert
when something needs attention.

## Alert levels

| Level | What it means | What to do |
| ----- | ------------- | ---------- |
| Info | Something to know, no action now | Nothing; it is listed in the vehicle history |
| Plan service | A part will need attention soon | Book a workshop visit within 14 days |
| Stop safely | Continuing could damage the truck | Stop as soon as it is safe and call roadside assistance |

## Service due dates

The "Service" tab shows the next maintenance date for each truck, calculated from mileage,
engine hours and battery use. You can book the visit with your preferred dealer from the same
tab.

## Who gets the alerts?

Fleet managers receive alerts by e-mail and in the app. You can add drivers or workshop
contacts per vehicle under "Settings > Notifications". Alerts are shown in the language set
for each user.
