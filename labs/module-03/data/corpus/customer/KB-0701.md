# Charging your ED-26 at the depot

## AC or DC?

Your ED-26 can charge in two ways:

| Charger | Maximum power | Typical time |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| AC wallbox (Type 2) | 22 kW | about 16 hours from 10 % to 100 % |
| DC fast charger (CCS) | 300 kW | about **55 minutes from 20 % to 80 %** |

Overnight AC charging is gentler on the battery and usually cheaper. Use DC fast charging
between shifts.

## Before a DC fast charge

Start **battery preconditioning** in the Fleet Insight app or from the instrument cluster
about 30 minutes before you arrive at the charger. A battery at the right temperature charges
at full power; a cold battery charges more slowly.

## Good habits

- Charge to 80 % for daily use and to 100 % only before long routes.
- Keep the charging cable off the ground and the connector cap closed.
- The truck cannot be driven while a cable is connected, even if charging has finished.

If the charging power is much lower than expected on a warm battery, note any message in the
instrument cluster and contact your dealer.
