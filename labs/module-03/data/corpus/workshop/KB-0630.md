# Parked regeneration of the diesel particulate filter on LH-40 tractors

## When it is needed

The diesel particulate filter (DPF) normally cleans itself while driving on the motorway.
Vehicles in short-distance or idle-heavy use cannot regenerate while driving. The instrument
cluster then shows the DPF lamp and fault code **FC-DPF-0310** (DPF soot load high).

## Conditions before you start

- Vehicle parked outdoors, away from anything that can burn: the exhaust gets very hot.
- Parking brake applied, gearbox in neutral, power take-off (PTO) switched off.
- Coolant temperature above 70 °C.
- At least a quarter of a tank of fuel.

## Procedure

1. Start the regeneration from the cluster menu "Service > DPF regeneration" or from DiagLink.
2. Engine speed rises to about 1,400 rpm. Do not touch the accelerator.
3. The regeneration takes **about 40 minutes**. Stopping it early leaves the filter loaded.
4. When the DPF lamp goes out, read the soot load in DiagLink: it should be below 20 %.

## If it keeps coming back

Three parked regenerations within 500 km point to a root cause such as a leaking injector or
an EGR fault. Diagnose it before the next regeneration.
