# Replacing the AdBlue pump filter during maintenance

## Interval

Replace the AdBlue pump filter at every second maintenance service, or every **240,000 km**,
whichever comes first. Vehicles that often run on AdBlue from unknown sources should have the
filter replaced at every service.

## Parts and tools

- Filter kit with seal and compensation element, part number **PN 47-0120-005**.
- Filter wrench 36 mm, a drip tray, and protective gloves and goggles: AdBlue irritates the
  eyes.

## Procedure

1. Switch off the ignition and wait 2 minutes so the pump empties the dosing line back into
   the tank.
2. Unscrew the filter cover from the bottom of the pump unit and drain the remaining AdBlue.
3. Remove the old filter and the compensation element. Do not reuse either part.
4. Fit the new compensation element and filter, lightly wet the seal with AdBlue, and tighten
   the cover to **20 Nm**.
5. Switch on the ignition and let the pump prime the system. Check the cover for leaks.

Record the filter change in the service booklet and in the Dealer Workshop Portal.
