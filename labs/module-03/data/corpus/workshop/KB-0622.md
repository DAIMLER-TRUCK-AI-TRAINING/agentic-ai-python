# Fault code FC-EAX-1310: eAxle coolant flow low

## What the code means

The coolant flow in the eAxle and battery cooling circuit is below **12 litres per minute**.
The nominal flow is 18 litres per minute with the pump at full speed. The eAxle EAX-2 reduces
its power output while the fault is active.

## Checks

1. **Coolant level.** With the circuit cold, the level in the expansion tank must be between
   MIN and MAX. Top up only with the approved coolant mixture.
2. **Air in the circuit.** Run the DiagLink routine **"EAX coolant bleed"**. It runs the pump
   in cycles for about 12 minutes; keep the expansion tank cap closed.
3. **Pump connector.** Check the electric coolant pump connector for corrosion and a correct
   lock. A loose connector gives intermittent faults.
4. **Software.** At outside temperatures below −5 °C, a false FC-EAX-1310 is fixed by the
   battery management software update in **TSB-2026-047**.

## When to replace the pump

Replace the coolant pump only if the flow stays below 12 litres per minute after bleeding,
with a good connector and current software. Record the measured flow in the warranty claim.
