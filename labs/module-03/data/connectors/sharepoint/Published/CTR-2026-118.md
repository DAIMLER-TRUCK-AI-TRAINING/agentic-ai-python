# Fleet uptime service contract CTR-2026-118

## §1 Parties and vehicles

Service contract between the company and fleet customer **CUST-40817** for 120 ED-26 and 60
LH-40 trucks operated in the EU, valid from 1 March 2026 for 5 years.

## §2 Uptime guarantee

The company guarantees an uptime of **97 % per quarter** for each vehicle group (ED-26 and
LH-40). Downtime starts when the customer reports a breakdown and ends when the vehicle is
ready for use again.

## §3 Response times

- Breakdown assistance on site within **2 hours** inside the contract area.
- A replacement vehicle if a repair is expected to take longer than **48 hours**.

## §4 Service credits

For each 0.5 percentage point of uptime below 97 % in a quarter, the customer receives a credit
of **2 % of the monthly service fee** for that vehicle group. Credits are capped at **15 %** of
the quarterly fee.

## §5 Exclusions

Downtime caused by accidents, driver misuse, missing charging infrastructure at the customer's
depot, or maintenance appointments the customer cancelled does not count against the guarantee.
