# Standard vehicle warranty: EU and EEA (2025 edition, superseded)

## §1 Scope

This policy applies to LH-40 and CT-33 trucks first registered in the EU or the EEA between
1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025.

## §2 Vehicle warranty

Every vehicle is covered for **24 months from the date of first registration**, with no
mileage limit.

## §3 Driveline warranty

Engine, gearbox and axles are covered for **24 months or 300,000 km**, whichever comes first.

## §4 Exclusions

Wear parts, damage caused by fluids that do not meet the specification, and defects caused by
late maintenance.

## §5 Claims

The dealer submits the claim within **60 days** of the repair.
