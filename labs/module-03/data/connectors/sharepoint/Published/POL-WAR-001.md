# Standard vehicle warranty: EU and EEA (2026 edition)

## §1 Scope

This policy applies to LH-40, CT-33 and ED-26 trucks first registered in the EU or the EEA on
or after 1 January 2026. Vehicles registered earlier stay under the edition in force at their
first registration.

## §2 Vehicle warranty

Every vehicle is covered for **24 months from the date of first registration**, with no
mileage limit. The warranty covers material and workmanship defects.

## §3 Driveline warranty

Engine, gearbox, axles and the eAxle are covered for **36 months or 450,000 km**, whichever
comes first.

## §4 Exclusions

- Wear parts: brake pads and discs, clutch linings, wiper blades and tyres.
- Damage caused by fluids that do not meet the specification, for example diluted or
  contaminated AdBlue.
- Defects caused by maintenance that was not carried out on time.
- The high-voltage battery of the ED-26, which is covered by POL-WAR-007.

## §5 Claims

The dealer submits the claim in the Dealer Workshop Portal within **30 days** of the repair.
Replaced parts must be kept for **60 days** for a possible inspection.
