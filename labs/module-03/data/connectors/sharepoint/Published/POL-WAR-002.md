# Standard vehicle warranty: United States (2026 edition)

## §1 Scope

This policy applies to LH-40, CT-33 and ED-26 trucks sold and first registered in the United
States on or after 1 January 2026.

## §2 Vehicle warranty

Every vehicle is covered for **24 months from the delivery date**, with no mileage limit.

## §3 Driveline warranty

Engine, transmission, axles and the eAxle are covered for **60 months or 500,000 miles
(about 800,000 km)**, whichever comes first.

## §4 Exclusions

- Wear parts: brake linings, clutch, wiper blades and tires.
- Damage caused by fluids that do not meet the specification, including diesel exhaust fluid
  (DEF) that is diluted or contaminated.
- Defects caused by maintenance that was not carried out on time.
- The high-voltage battery of the ED-26, which is covered by POL-WAR-007.

## §5 Claims

The dealer submits the claim in the Dealer Workshop Portal within **45 days** of the repair.
