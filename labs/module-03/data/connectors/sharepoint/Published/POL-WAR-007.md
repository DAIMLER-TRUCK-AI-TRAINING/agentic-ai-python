# High-voltage battery warranty for the ED-26

## §1 Scope

This policy covers the high-voltage (HV) battery packs of ED-26 battery-electric trucks in all
regions.

## §2 Coverage period

The HV battery is covered for **8 years or 800,000 km**, whichever comes first, from the date
of first registration.

## §3 Capacity guarantee

During the coverage period the usable capacity of the battery stays at or above **70 %** of its
nominal capacity. If the DiagLink state-of-health test measures less than 70 %, the affected
battery modules are repaired or replaced free of charge.

## §4 Exclusions

- Damage from accidents, flooding or fire.
- Damage from chargers that do not comply with the CCS standard.
- Battery packs opened or repaired by anyone without HV qualification level 2.

## §5 Claims

Attach the DiagLink state-of-health report and the battery management log to the claim.
Faults such as FC-BAT-2201 must be diagnosed according to the workshop knowledge base before a
module is replaced.
