# Goodwill policy for repairs after the warranty period

## §1 Purpose

Goodwill is a voluntary contribution to a repair after the warranty has expired. It protects
customer relationships; it is never a legal obligation.

## §2 Conditions

A goodwill request is possible only if the vehicle is **no more than 12 months beyond the end
of its warranty**, all maintenance was carried out on time and documented, and the defect is not
a wear part.

## §3 Approval limits

| Contribution per case | Who approves |
| --------------------- | ------------ |
| Up to €2,500 (US: $2,700) | The dealer's service manager |
| Above €2,500 and up to €25,000 | The regional warranty team |
| Above €25,000 | The Head of After-Sales |

## §4 Documentation

Every goodwill decision is recorded in the Dealer Workshop Portal with the reason. Decisions
above the dealer limit need the regional warranty team's case number before the repair starts.

## §5 Customer communication

Do not describe goodwill as warranty in letters to customers, and do not promise goodwill
before it has been approved.
