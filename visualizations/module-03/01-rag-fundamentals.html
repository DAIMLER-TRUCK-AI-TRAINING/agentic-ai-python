A search index in front of a generator

The model has never read your knowledge base. You can ignore that, paste the whole KB into every prompt, train the facts into the weights, or retrieve the few passages that matter at question time. Engineering takeaway: RAG is a search index in front of a generator. The generator can only be as good as
    what the search returns, so most RAG engineering is retrieval engineering: chunking (Topic 4), metadata and hybrid search (Topic 5)
    and measurement (Topic 6). The model call is the easy part.

  
Common trap: believing that RAG removes hallucination. It lowers the odds by putting the right
    passage in front of the model, but the model can still ignore it, blend it with its own priors, or answer when nothing relevant was
    retrieved. Require a citation for every claim, check that the cited ID was actually retrieved, and let the assistant say “not in
    the knowledge base”.
