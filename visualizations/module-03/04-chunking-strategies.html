Topic 4

Where you cut decides what the model can read

The model only sees the chunks you retrieve. Cut a policy in the wrong place and the rule travels without its exception. Ask “Can a contractor use a personal laptop on the VPN?” against POL-SEC-012 (the right answer is “yes, with MDM enrolment and written sponsor approval”). Start with fixed 300-character chunks, then try overlap, semantic breakpoints and hierarchical parents until the answer is right without sending the whole document.

Engineering takeaway: chunking is a retrieval setting, so tune it like one. Pick the strategy for the
    document type (headings → structure-aware or hierarchical; free text → semantic; logs and tickets → fixed), carry the section
    heading into every chunk, then measure recall on labelled questions (Topic 6) before and after each change.

  
Common trap: “bigger chunks are safer”. At 600 characters every chunk holds more of the policy, but
    each one now mixes topics, so its embedding matches every question a little and none of them well, and every answer pays for
    three times the tokens. Overlap is no cure either: 400 characters with 100 overlap loses the exception that 400 with none keeps.
