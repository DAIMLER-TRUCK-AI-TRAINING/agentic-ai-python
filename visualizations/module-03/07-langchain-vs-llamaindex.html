Same pipeline, different defaults

LangChain and LlamaIndex build the same six-stage RAG pipeline with different classes, abstractions and defaults. Toggle the framework and click each stage to compare the code. Then run three test questions with each framework’s defaults, and switch on Align settings: most of the “framework difference” in answer quality was the defaults all along.

Engineering takeaway: compare frameworks with aligned settings and one eval set (Topic 6): same
    chunking, embedding model, k and prompt. Then choose on developer experience, connectors and how retrieval fits the rest of your
    system. Mixing is normal: LlamaIndex ingestion and retrieval wrapped as a tool in a LangChain or LangGraph agent works well.

  
Common trap: shipping a framework’s defaults. They were picked for quick starts, not for your
    documents: LlamaIndex’s top 2 misses multi-part answers, and LangChain’s 4,000-character chunks send whole articles. Set
    chunk size, overlap and k explicitly, and record them with your eval results.
