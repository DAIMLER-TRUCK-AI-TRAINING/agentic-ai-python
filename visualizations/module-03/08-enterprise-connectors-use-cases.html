One pattern, many sources, four assistants

Enterprise RAG lives or dies at the connectors: what they load, which permissions come with it, and how changes and deletions reach the index. Engineering takeaway: write one normalisation step that turns every reader’s output into the same
    document shape: doc_id, source, url, last_modified, status and
    allowed_groups. Everything downstream (chunking, filters, citations, incremental sync and the offline fallback) then
    works the same for every source, and a new use case is configuration, not new code.

  
Common trap: syncing additions and edits but not deletions or permission changes. A page that was
    deleted, or restricted to Legal yesterday, keeps answering questions from the index today. Re-sync permissions with content, and
    remove or tombstone chunks whose source has gone.
