Pick the smallest circle that solves the problem

AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI are nested, not competing, and every step inward buys flexibility at the price of cost, latency and determinism. Click each IT-ops use case to drop it into the smallest circle that can solve it, then switch to the enterprise stack and swap the model provider.

Which circle, when?

FieldLearns fromIT-ops exampleStrengthWatch out for
Rule-based AINothing; humans write the rulesKeyword routing, escalation matricesDeterministic, auditable, free to runBrittle when wording varies

Generative AIMassive text/code corpora, then instruction tuningIncident summaries, draft replies, agentsOne model handles open-ended language tasksNon-deterministic, costs per token, can hallucinate (Topic 5)
Foundation modelPre-trained once on broad data, adapted many waysGPT and Claude for generation; BERT-style encoders for embeddingsReuse instead of training from scratchKnows nothing about your internal systems unless you supply it
Engineering takeaway: before reaching for an LLM, ask whether a rule, a classical model or a small encoder already solves the task. Each step inward adds per-call cost, latency and output you must validate, so spend it only where open-ended language actually earns its keep.

Four ways to adapt a foundation model

ApproachWhat changesCost to updateWhere in this course
PromptingInstructions and examples in each request; the model is untouchedMinutes: edit a templateModule 2
Retrieval (RAG)Relevant documents are fetched and added to the context at call timeRe-index changed documentsModule 3
Fine-tuningThe model's weights, trained on your examplesA training run plus re-evaluationModule 3 (decision framework)
Tools and agentsThe model calls your APIs inside a loop with guardrailsDeploy or change a tool contractModules 2 and 4
Common trap: assuming a foundation model “was trained on everything”, so it must know KB-0042 or your change calendar. It saw broad public data up to a cutoff date. Your internal facts reach it only through the prompt, retrieval or fine-tuning, so plan that path explicitly.
