Topic 2

Every NLP stage inherits the last one’s mistakes

A classic NLP pipeline turns a support ticket into a routing decision in five cheap, deterministic stages. Step a ticket through tokenisation, POS tagging, NER, semantic similarity and intent detection. Then switch off the custom entity rules and re-run ticket A or C to see a confident route collapse into manual triage.

Engineering takeaway: classic NLP stages are cheap, deterministic and unit-testable. Use them as pre-filters and features, keep a regression set of tickets for every custom rule, and send only the ambiguous tail to an LLM. That keeps cost and latency down on the 80 % of tickets a rule already handles.
Common trap: sizing LLM cost from word counts. The pipeline sees 12 words in ticket A; a subword tokenizer sees more (16 in this toy split), because IDs and rare words break into pieces. Count tokens with the provider’s tokenizer or token-counting endpoint before you set a budget.
