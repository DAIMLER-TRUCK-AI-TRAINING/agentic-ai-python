Topic 7

The cheapest model that clears your bar, under your constraints

There is no “best model”, only the cheapest tier that meets the scenario’s quality bar, latency budget and data-residency rule, with Responsible AI checks built in rather than bolted on. Pick one of four IT scenarios, adjust the constraints, click a point to see why it passes or fails, and tick the checks until the decision record says ready.

Engineering takeaway: decide with your own eval set, not leaderboards. Filter by hard constraints
    (residency, latency), then pick the cheapest tier that clears the quality bar on your tickets, and re-run that eval whenever a model
    version changes. Model IDs change often, so the labs read them from configuration (OPENAI_MODEL, ANTHROPIC_MODEL).

  
Common trap: “use the most capable model to be safe.” In scenario A the frontier tier costs 25× the
    small tier and still misses the 1.5 s latency budget. Over-provisioning is a cost and latency bug, not a safety measure.
