Characters are not fields

Engineering takeaway: treat OCR output like untrusted input. Validate every field you act on:
    line items must sum to the total, IDs must match their pattern (^INV-\d{5}$, ^INC-\d{6}$), dates must
    parse. Route anything that fails to a human queue instead of into the ERP.

Common trap: one confidence threshold for every engine. Tesseract and Textract report 0–100,
    Azure reports 0–1. A raw “≥ 0.9” rule accepts every Tesseract word. Normalise first, then calibrate the threshold per
    engine on your own documents.
